Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Scholin ("we", "our", "us") provides a school management system for primary and secondary schools in Kenya. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, role (admin, teacher, student, parent), and school affiliation.
  • School records — student grades, attendance, timetables, and related academic data entered by authorised school staff.
  • Communication data — messages sent through Scholin's messaging feature.
  • Usage data — device type, app version, and basic diagnostic information to improve reliability.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and deliver the Scholin service to your school.
  • To authenticate users and enforce role-based access.
  • To enable communication between teachers, students, and parents.
  • To generate academic reports and attendance summaries.
  • To detect and prevent misuse or unauthorised access.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your data. Information is shared only with:

  • Authorised users within your school (based on their role).
  • Service providers that help us run Scholin (hosting, payments), bound by confidentiality.
  • Legal authorities, if required by Kenyan law.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on secure servers with encryption in transit. Access is restricted to authorised personnel only. We retain data for as long as your school uses Scholin.

5. Children's Privacy

Scholin stores student data on behalf of schools. Schools are responsible for obtaining any parental consent required under Kenyan law. Parents may contact their school to review or request changes to their child's data.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting your school administrator or us directly at support@scholin.ke.

7. Payments

Payments made through Scholin are processed by third-party payment providers (such as Paystack). We do not store full card details on our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Scholin after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions? Email support@scholin.ke.
